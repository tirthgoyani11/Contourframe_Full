Explore the studio

Build a route explainer, a historical sequence, or a short map story. Start with the map, then add motion and context as your story develops.

Camera moves and timeline

Use top-down, fly-to, orbit, or cinematic sweep transitions. Set where each move starts and ends, adjust its duration, and scrub the timeline to preview the timing.

Map styles and highlights

Switch between light, dark, and satellite basemaps. Group countries into scenes and use fills and borders to direct attention as the story moves across the map.

Route

Travel routes and annotations

Draw a route or import a GPX track. Add labels, arrows, images, and map icons to explain where things happened and how locations connect.

Motion stickers and 3D models

Browse animated stickers by category or search for a specific overlay. Place 3D objects on the map to illustrate vehicles, buildings, and landmarks in a scene.

Historical maps and changing borders

Explore the historical boundary layers included in the editor. Animate territorial changes across scenes to show how a boundary shifts over time.
