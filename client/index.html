War morphNEW

Free for everyone
–

Borders now move with their own shape. When a country takes land, part of the border stays put and the rest walks to the new edge, carrying its own outline. The losing side pulls back along the same line.

It has its own tool in the toolbar, with three modes: whole country, province and pen. Your existing war tool is unchanged.

This tool is still in development. You may run into bugs.

3D modelsNEW

Free for everyone
–

Forty 3D objects you can place on the map: tanks, ships, aircraft, trains, cars, siege engines and map props. They stand where you put them, or travel between two scenes, driving, sailing, flying and firing as they go.

They have their own button in the toolbar. Open it and pick one to start.

Using 3D models

Standing still. Pick a model and it lands in the scene you are on. Drag it into place, then set size, heading and colour in the panel on the right.

Making it travel. A model moves when the SAME model sits in two scenes at different spots.

1. Copy the object. Select it, Ctrl+C, open the next scene, Ctrl+V, drag the copy to where it should arrive.

2. Copy the scene. Right-click the scene in the timeline, choose Duplicate, then move the model in the copy.

Do not add it twice. Picking the same model from the library again gives you two separate models, and both stand still. The link comes from copying.

Pasting into the same scene gives a second model instead of movement. How it travels comes from the movement mode: vehicles curve, aircraft take off and land, ships route over water, the train lays its own track.
